🌱 DAILY ACTIONS

Simple Green Tips

Small changes in our daily habits can create a meaningful impact.

💡

Save Electricity

Turn off lights, fans and appliances when they are not needed.

🚰

Save Water

Fix leaking taps and avoid wasting water while brushing.

🛍️

Avoid Single-Use Plastic

Carry reusable bags and bottles whenever possible.

🚲

Choose Green Transport

Walk, cycle or use public transport for shorter journeys.

🌳

Plant Trees

Support tree planting and protect existing green spaces.

🥗

Reduce Food Waste

Plan meals and store food properly to avoid unnecessary waste.
